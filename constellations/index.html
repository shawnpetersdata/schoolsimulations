This is a schematic model: constellation patterns are recognizable approximations, not a precision star chart. Visibility and sky positions use simplified celestial coordinates and ideal horizons. The fixed celestial sphere and constellation patterns are schematic, not to scale. Stars fade during daytime because sunlight brightens the sky, not because the stars move or switch off.
