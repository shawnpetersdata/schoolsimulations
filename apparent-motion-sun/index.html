Investigate: Compare the daylight hours for different latitudes in December, March, June, and September. Follow the observer as Earth rotates. How do the Sun's path and the length of the day change?

Simplified model: 24-hour solar day, fixed axial tilt, ideal horizon and no atmospheric refraction. Earth, Sun and distances are not to scale. Local solar noon is 12:00 PM. The orbit view is schematic and shows the four seasonal positions, not the scale or duration of the orbit; the sky graph shows the Sun's altitude over time rather than its compass bearing.
