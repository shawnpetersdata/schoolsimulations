Virtual Flower Dissection

Biology Lab · Alstroemeria

Instructions

  1. Examine the flower before beginning your dissection.
  2. Click and drag individual structures away from the flower. Arrange them in the appropriate labelled sections of the workspace.
  3. When you are ready to dissect the pistil, press C to enter cutting mode.
  4. Click and drag to make a lengthwise cut through the centre of the pistil. Release the mouse to complete the cut.
  5. If the cut does not open the pistil, inspect its placement and try again. Press C to return to moving mode if needed.
  6. Examine the exposed structures. Drag exposed parts away and place them in the appropriate section.
  7. Finish arranging the dissected parts in the labelled workspace, then press S to save an image of your completed work.
Mouse: Select and drag C Toggle cutting mode S Save workspace image

Work carefully. The cutting tool requires a proper cut to expose the internal structures.
