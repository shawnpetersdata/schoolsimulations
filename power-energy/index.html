Suggested investigation questions

  1. Set the mass to 10 kg and the height to 1.5 m. Compare a 90° vertical lift with a 30° ramp. Which method requires less force?
  2. Which method requires moving the object a greater distance?
  3. Calculate the work done in each case. What do you notice?
  4. Change the ramp angle to 15°, then 45°, then 90°. How do the force and distance change? What happens to the total work?
  5. At the same height, what happens to gravitational potential energy when the object's mass is doubled?
  6. During the motion, the object has kinetic energy. Does its kinetic energy increase when it travels at constant speed? Explain.
